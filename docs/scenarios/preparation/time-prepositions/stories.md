# 時間介系詞圖解：IN／ON／AT

s01. Look at three words: in, on, and at.

看看這三個字：in、on 和 at。

s02. Use in with years, months, and seasons.

年、月和季節使用 in。

s03. We say in winter and in autumn.

我們說 in winter（在冬天）和 in autumn（在秋天）。

s04. We also say in 2024 and in April.

我們也說 in 2024（在二〇二四年）和 in April（在四月）。

s05. For parts of the day, say in the morning, in the afternoon, and in the evening.

一天中的時段可以說 in the morning（在早上）、in the afternoon（在下午）和 in the evening（在傍晚或晚上）。

s06. Now, on.

接著是 on。

s07. Use on with days and dates.

星期和日期使用 on。

s08. Say on Monday and on December 25.

說 on Monday（在星期一）和 on December 25（在十二月二十五日）。

s09. We also say on my birthday.

我們也說 on my birthday（在我的生日當天）。

s10. For a morning on one day, say on Monday morning.

指定某一天的早上時，可以說 on Monday morning（在星期一早上）。

s11. Next, at.

接著是 at。

s12. Use at with a time on the clock.

時鐘上的時間點使用 at。

s13. Say at five in the afternoon and at seven in the morning.

說 at five in the afternoon（在下午五點）和 at seven in the morning（在早上七點），對應圖上的 at 5:00 p.m. 和 at 7:00 a.m.。

s14. We also say at noon, at midnight, and at night.

我們也說 at noon（在中午）、at midnight（在午夜）和 at night（在夜晚）。

s15. These words help us talk about time.

這些字幫助我們表達時間。

s16. Some time words do not need in, on, or at.

有些時間表達不用加 in、on 或 at。

s17. Say this morning and next Monday.

說 this morning（今天早上）和 next Monday（下星期一）。

s18. Also say last year and every day.

也可以說 last year（去年）和 every day（每天）。

s19. Do not put in, on, or at before these four time phrases.

這四種時間片語前面不加 in、on 或 at。

# 時間介系詞圖解：先後、持續與期限

s01. Look at more words for time.

看看更多表達時間的字。

s02. Before lunch means earlier than lunch.

before lunch 表示在午餐之前。

s03. After lunch means later than lunch.

after lunch 表示在午餐之後。

s04. From and to show the start and end.

from 和 to 分別指出起點和終點。

s05. Say from nine in the morning to five in the afternoon.

說 from nine in the morning to five in the afternoon，表示從早上九點到下午五點。

s06. Finish by five means finish at five or earlier.

Finish by five 表示最晚五點完成，也可以提早完成。

s07. Work until five means keep working up to five.

Work until five 表示持續工作到五點。

s08. By shows when to finish, but until shows how long to keep working.

by 指出完成期限，until 則指出持續工作到何時。

s09. For two hours tells us how long.

for two hours 告訴我們持續多久：兩小時。

s10. I have been here since Monday.

我從星期一起就一直在這裡。

s11. Since Monday tells us when the time started.

since Monday 告訴我們開始的時間是星期一。

s12. It continues from Monday to now.

這個狀態從星期一持續到現在。

s13. We can talk during the meeting.

我們可以在會議期間交談。

s14. During tells us when, but for tells us how long.

during 說明在哪段期間內，for 則說明持續多久。

s15. I will be back in two hours.

我會在兩小時後回來。

s16. Here, in two hours means two hours from now.

這裡的 in two hours 表示從現在算起兩小時後。

s17. I will be back within two hours.

我會在兩小時內回來。

s18. Within two hours means no later than two hours from now.

within two hours 表示最晚在從現在算起的兩小時內。

s19. I can be back earlier, too.

我也可以提早回來。
