STYLE
Use case: illustration-story. ONE landscape 16:9 interactive English-learning base illustration. High-quality adorable 3D cartoon, rounded appealing characters, soft materials and warm natural sunlight. Match the cozy, polished family-friendly visual language of a warm hotel check-in learning illustration. Opaque background.

SCENE DESCRIPTION
An airport departure gate during boarding, shown as one coherent wide scene. A female gate agent checks a boy's travel document at a gate desk, several passengers wait in an orderly queue, and a DIFFERENT passenger walks through the gate entrance into a visible enclosed jet bridge. Through large terminal windows, a large parked passenger airplane is clearly recognizable.

TARGET VOCABULARY
Show visual anchors for airport, airplane, gate, ticket, passenger, bag, window, seat, line (queue), screen, clock, uniform, busy, heavy, empty, ready, wait, carry, check, show, board. Also allow natural supporting details of a passport and rolling suitcase, without making labels or written answers.

OBJECT VISIBILITY RULES
The airplane must have a clear passenger-airliner silhouette outside the broad glass windows, with fuselage, wings, engines and tail visible enough to recognize it. The entrance into the enclosed boarding corridor must be obvious and connected visually to the aircraft, not look like an unrelated doorway.
A boy clearly SHOWS a small blank paper travel document to a uniformed female gate agent who CHECKS it with a small handheld reader. Keep the paper plainly visible and unobstructed, with no text, numbers or barcode. The vocabulary ticket means the general paper travel document in this picture; do not depict boarding using a theater-style admission ticket.
Behind the boy, an adult passenger CARRIES a visibly bulging travel duffel with both hands and a mildly effortful bent-elbow posture so HEAVY is visually supported. A woman behind him completes a readable orderly queue. Keep the bag raised from the floor, not a suitcase being rolled. Nearby place a separate rolling suitcase as a natural supporting prop.
Show a complete row of at least two clearly EMPTY airport seats at the left foreground, not blocked by people.
Above the waiting area show an analog CLOCK with hands and simple tick marks, with no numerals. Place it clearly ABOVE a wall-mounted SCREEN. The screen is a real rectangular display showing only abstract airplane pictograms and simple colored bars, absolutely no text or digits.
The agent wears an obvious navy-and-gold professional UNIFORM. Travelers hold their things and face the gate, looking READY to depart. BUSY comes from the simultaneous checking, queueing and walking, not clutter.

COMPOSITION
Wide landscape 16:9 from a slightly elevated three-quarter camera. Empty seats and the passenger carrying the heavy bag on the left; the queue and agent desk in the middle; open gate and walking boarding passenger at the right; large aircraft outside windows across the background. Put the clock and screen in the upper left/middle where both are fully visible. Keep key objects in frame, separated, large enough for future interactive labels, with room on the floor, walls and counter for website overlays. Do not use a collage or panels.

CHARACTERS / ACTIONS
Five principal characters: smiling adult female gate agent; boy showing his paper document at the desk; adult man behind him carrying the heavy duffel; adult woman waiting behind the man; and a separate adult passenger already walking through the gate into the jet bridge. Each character has one coherent action. Natural, distinct hands and feet. The gate agent and boy should face each other. The boarding traveler walks away into the gate, not toward the waiting line.

ANNOTATION STYLE
Clean art only. The website adds all interactive labels and arrows later. No rendered annotations.

TEXT RULES
NO readable text, letters, numerals, vocabulary labels, gate numbers, captions, signs, logos, airline branding, watermarks or speech bubbles anywhere. No writing on plane, screens, uniforms, passports or papers. Analog clock uses ticks only, screen uses pictograms only. Do not add arrows or explanatory callouts.

DECORATION
Bright clean contemporary airport, gentle cream and pastel-blue tones, warm sunlight, rounded chairs and tidy architecture. Secondary details support the airport without competing with teaching objects.

NEGATIVE REQUIREMENTS
No security checkpoint, luggage carousel, aircraft interior or meal service. No mystery doorway disconnected from the airplane. No tiny distant airplane. No paper covered with fake writing. No missing clock/screen/empty seat/heavy bag. No giant explanatory symbols, no split screen, no photographic humans, no dark shadows or clutter. No fused hands or impossible body positions.
