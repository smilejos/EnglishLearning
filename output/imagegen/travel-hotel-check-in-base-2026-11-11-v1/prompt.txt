STYLE
Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE polished landscape 16:9 base illustration for an interactive English-learning scene. Warm, bright, adorable high-quality 3D cartoon rendering, rounded appealing characters, soft tactile materials, sunny cream-and-wood hotel interior, cheerful restrained colors. Opaque background. A single coherent hotel lobby, not a collage or infographic.

SCENE DESCRIPTION
A welcoming hotel front desk during check-in. Show four naturally connected activity areas in one spacious room: central reception counter, luggage storage and porter on the left, self-service refreshment table on the right, and a neat amenity shelf/tray in the near-right foreground.

TARGET VOCABULARY
Visually support all these concepts, without drawing their words: receptionist, guest, front desk, passport, key card, bell, form, pen, reservation, luggage, suitcase, cart, tag, water, tea, coffee, cup, toothbrush, toothpaste, towel, shampoo, soap, friendly, porter, check in, leave (temporarily leaving luggage with hotel staff), ask, give, sign.

OBJECT VISIBILITY RULES
Each important object must be large and clearly distinguishable. On the central counter show a small OPEN passport booklet, one plain plastic key card being handed to a guest, a shiny dome service bell, a blank ruled check-in form and a distinct pen in a signing guest's hand. A guest's smartphone on the counter shows only a simple bed pictogram with a checkmark to suggest a reservation, no letters or numbers.
On the left show a recognizable wheeled hotel luggage cart, a group of travel bags, one separate hard-shell rolling suitcase, and a clearly visible large blank luggage tag tied to its handle. A uniformed porter is receiving or attaching the tag to that suitcase. This is a hotel luggage storage area, not an airport conveyor.
On the right refreshment table show a transparent clear-water pitcher, a recognizable teapot with amber tea, a separate glass coffee carafe containing dark coffee, and a small row of empty handled cups. The water, tea, coffee and cups are separate, unobstructed.
In the near-right foreground, a tidy guest amenity shelf/tray presents FIVE distinct essentials: a toothbrush with visible bristles in a clear sleeve, a toothpaste squeeze tube placed next to the toothbrush, neatly folded towels, a small unlabeled shampoo bottle, and an oval soap bar in an open simple wrapper. They must be prominent and readable as silhouettes, not tiny bottles in the distant background. Use foreground perspective for their visibility; keep believable proportions.

COMPOSITION
Wide landscape 16:9 view from a slightly elevated three-quarter camera so we see the countertop and tray surfaces. Reception occupies the central half; porter and luggage occupy left quarter; tea station and near-foreground amenities occupy right quarter at different depths. Keep all important objects within frame with comfortable margins. Use the room's floor, counter and shelving to connect all areas naturally, no drawn borders or separate panels. Balance people and props with breathing room for future website overlays. Do not let bodies block the passport, bell, signing form, key card handoff, tag, or amenity tray.

CHARACTERS / ACTIONS
Friendly professional adult female receptionist behind the main counter smiles while handing a key card to an adult woman guest. A DIFFERENT adult guest beside her signs the check-in form with a pen. Do not make a single guest perform all actions at once. A uniformed adult porter on the left handles the tagged suitcase beside the cart. Near the amenity shelf, a second hotel staff member offers a toothbrush to a child who is politely asking for it. Six characters total, coherent anatomy and natural hand poses. Each action has its own space. The family and staff look relaxed, welcoming, and engaged.

ANNOTATION STYLE
No annotations in the rendered art. The application adds interactive labels later. The base image must be clean.

TEXT RULES
Absolutely NO readable text, letters, numbers, vocabulary labels, captions, hotel name, signage, brand marks, watermarks, arrows or speech bubbles anywhere. Passport, luggage tag, shampoo bottle, toothpaste tube, paper forms, card and devices have blank or simple abstract surfaces. The phone may contain a bed pictogram and checkmark ONLY. Do not turn target vocabulary into written labels.

DECORATION
Simple sunlit hotel architecture with warm wood and soft furnishings in the background. Minimal decoration, just enough to feel like a real welcoming hotel. Keep the foreground teaching objects crisp, well lit and visible.

NEGATIVE REQUIREMENTS
No text or baked-in answer words. No infographic layout, no floating object catalog, no split screen, no photo-realism, no dark lighting, no clutter or large empty ceiling, no mechanical room, no airport equipment. No physical metal room key instead of the key card. No duplicated or fused objects. No extra fingers or merged hands. Do not omit the porter, luggage tag, toothbrush, toothpaste, towel, shampoo or soap.
