SCENE DESCRIPTION
A large passenger ferry ALREADY UNDER WAY at sea, viewed from a slightly elevated exterior three-quarter angle near its open passenger deck. We can see the occupied upper deck, the forward wheelhouse with captain, and a meaningful portion of the ferry's outside white-and-blue hull plus the surrounding sea. It must read as riding a large ferry, not standing on an anonymous wooden pier.

TARGET VOCABULARY
Show ship, boat (a DIFFERENT smaller craft), passenger, captain, sailor, sea, island, wave, rope, flag, bench, bag, sunny, windy, large, sail (navigate under way), sit, hold, watch. Natural supporting objects may include safety railings, deck, and a stowed life jacket, without turning them into labels.

OBJECT VISIBILITY RULES
The primary ferry is a substantial passenger SHIP; show enough white-and-blue hull and continuous deck structure to recognize the large vessel itself. The ferry is moving, with a visible white foamy wake trailing aft and gentle water ripples. There are NO sails or sailboat masts on the ferry.
A DISTINCT small motor BOAT is visible on the sea beside the ferry at a safe clear separation; it must be complete and large enough to identify, clearly much smaller than the ferry. The relative scale supports LARGE.
At the FRONT of the ferry show a small wheelhouse with the CAPTAIN visibly at the wheel through an open side or large unobstructed window; do not hide him behind opaque glass. A separate uniformed SAILOR is on the deck beside a neatly COILED ROPE.
The ferry has a clear FLAG fluttering in the wind, with simple solid colors and no text, crests or logos. Slightly windblown hair and clothing support WINDY, while the bright blue sky and warm sunlight support SUNNY.
An adult PASSENGER SITS on a fixed wooden BENCH on the open deck and HOLDS a brimmed hat on his head with one hand against the breeze. His other hand can rest naturally. A clearly visible travel BAG is secured beside the bench.
A child safely inside the railing WATCHES the sea and a green ISLAND ahead. Use body and gaze direction to point naturally toward the ocean, no drawn arrows.
Include clearly visible gentle WAVES beside the ferry, a green island with a small hill, and broad blue SEA. Keep shipboard life relaxed rather than stormy.

COMPOSITION
A single coherent wide 16:9 scene using a three-quarter exterior angle slightly above deck height so BOTH the large ferry hull and passengers on its open deck are visible. The forward wheelhouse and captain occupy the left/front side; sailor and coiled rope near it; bench, seated hat-holding adult and watching child on the central/right deck. Keep the lower portion of the ship in frame, with sea visible around it. The separate small motorboat and island sit in the open-water background to the right, away from the main characters. The flag and ship wake must be visible. Maintain believable vessel geometry, clear safety railings, separated teaching objects and open space for future website labels. Not a collage, not an inset, not a dock view.

CHARACTERS / ACTIONS
Four main figures: captain steering in the forward wheelhouse, adult sailor tending the coiled rope on deck, seated adult traveler holding his hat, and child watching the scenery while safely behind the railing. Each person has a distinct clear role and natural anatomy. The child is not climbing or leaning over the railing. No one is boarding or disembarking.

DECORATION
Warm sunshine on clean white-and-blue ferry surfaces, cheerful sea-blue water, rounded green island, a few soft clouds. Coastal holiday mood, not a luxury yacht party. Keep deck details simple and useful to the learning scene.

No rope floating in midair, no tiny faraway motorboat, no floating cropped ship. No unsafe child pose, no storm waves, no dark lighting, no anatomical errors, no fused hands.
